How to split a budget across funnel stages

35 / 40 / 10 / 15: the starting split we use for new projects.

When we start a new project, we split the budget into four parts. It isn't a fixed rule, but it's a good starting point that we adjust as results come in.

Awareness — 35%

Broad audiences and short videos, judged on reach, CPM and frequency. The goal is for as many people as possible to hear about you.

Consideration — 40%

Traffic, engagement and video views. This is where people compare you with others, so it gets the largest share.

Remarketing — 10%

At the same consideration stage, we show ads to people who already watched a video or visited the site.

Conversion — 15%

Leads and sales. After the first two weeks we review results and move budget to whatever performs best.

By Levan Kalmakhelidze, CMO
