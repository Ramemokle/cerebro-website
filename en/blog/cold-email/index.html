Cold emails that stay out of spam

Four rules for B2B email outreach that won't get your account blocked.

Cold email is still one of the cheapest ways to find B2B clients. Sent the wrong way, though, Gmail blocks the account fast.

Four rules

  • 20–40 emails a day from a new account
  • 1–3 minutes between emails
  • Never the same address twice
  • An easy way to opt out, such as "reply STOP"

Finally, personalize. The company name in the subject and a short, clear offer work far better than a long text.

By Levan Kalmakhelidze, CMO
