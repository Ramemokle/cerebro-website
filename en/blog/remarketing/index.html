Why remarketing belongs in consideration

Buying property takes months. Remarketing keeps you in mind the whole time.

Many advertisers only use remarketing at the very end: "come back and buy". That works for an online shop, but not for an apartment.

The buyer is still comparing

A home buyer spends weeks or months comparing layouts, prices, locations and installment plans.

What we show

  • Carousels of floor plans
  • Construction progress
  • Installment terms
  • Residents' stories

As a result, leads reach your sales team warmer and better informed.
